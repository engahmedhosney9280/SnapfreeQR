About QR Snap

QR Snap is a small, free tool for one job: turning a link or a line of text into a QR code you can download and use right away.

Why it exists

Most QR generators bury the tool behind sign-up forms, watermarks, or a paywall for basic customization. We built QR Snap to be the opposite — open it, type, download. No account, no email required.

How it works, technically

Every code is built directly in your browser using open, well-established QR encoding — nothing you type is sent to a server or stored. That also means the tool keeps working even if our site is briefly unavailable, and your content stays private by default.

Who it's for

QR Snap is used for things like linking a printed flyer to a website, sharing Wi-Fi details, putting a portfolio link on a business card, or adding a scannable link to packaging and event signage.
