What we collect

The text or link you enter into the generator is processed entirely in your browser and is never transmitted to our servers or stored by us. We don't require an account, and we don't attach your generated codes to any identity.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic (like page views), which does not include the content of any QR code you generate.

Advertising

Pages on this site may display ads served by third-party partners. Those partners may use cookies or similar technology to show relevant ads; you can control this through your browser's cookie settings.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies, limited to what's needed for the site and any ad partners to function. No cookie is used to identify you personally.
